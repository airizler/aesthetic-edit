<h3 style="margin-bottom: 15px; font-size: 1.3rem;">🛒 Buying Guide: What to Consider Before Buying</h3>
<p style="line-height: 1.8; color: #444; margin-bottom: 20px;">When investing in a low-profile mechanical board, choose your switch colors carefully. <strong>Red switches</strong> are linear, incredibly quiet, and smooth—making them ideal for shared office environments. <strong>Blue switches</strong> offer a highly distinct, loud, satisfying clicky snap, which is great for pure typing enthusiasts working alone. <strong>Brown switches</strong> provide a balanced middle-ground, offering a tactile bump without excessive click noise, which is universally recommended for most digital setups.</p>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 15px; font-size: 1.3rem;">⚖️ Alternatives to Consider</h3>
<p style="line-height: 1.8; color: #444; margin-bottom: 40px;">The closest premium competitor is the <strong>Logitech MX Keys Mini</strong>. While the MX Keys Mini offers excellent battery endurance and soft membrane keys, it completely lacks the satisfying mechanical travel and customization options of the K3. Another alternative is Apple's official <strong>Magic Keyboard</strong>, which features clean aesthetics but offers a very shallow typing depth at a much higher price tag. For true enthusiasts, Keychron remains the champion of performance and value.</p>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 20px; font-size: 1.3rem;">❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<div style="margin-bottom: 50px; line-height: 1.7; font-size: 1rem;">
  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>1. Can I use the Keychron K3 while it is charging?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">Yes, absolutely! You can flip the back switch to 'Cable' mode, plug it into your laptop via the included Type-C cable, and use it as a standard wired keyboard while it fully recharges background battery.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>2. What is the difference between Hot-Swappable and Non-Swappable versions?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">The hot-swappable version allows you to easily pull out switches and pop in a new type (like changing from quiet Reds to tactile Browns) without needing any technical soldering tools. The non-swappable version has factory-soldered switches that cannot be removed.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>3. How long does the battery realistically last?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">With backlighting turned off completely, it can easily last up to 90 hours on a single charge (around 2-3 weeks of normal use). With RGB lighting active at max brightness, it will typically last around 34-40 hours.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>4. Does it come with a battery percentage indicator?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 20px; color: #555;">Yes, a small LED light located right next to the charging port flashes red when the battery drops below 15%, letting you know exactly when it's time to plug in your cable.</p>
