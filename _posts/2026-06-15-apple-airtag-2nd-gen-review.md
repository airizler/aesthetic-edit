<h3 style="margin-bottom: 20px; font-size: 1.3rem;">❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<div style="margin-bottom: 50px; line-height: 1.7; font-size: 1rem;">
  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>1. Do I need to charge the AirTag?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">No, it uses a standard CR2032 coin cell battery that lasts over a year. When it runs low, your iPhone will notify you, and you can easily replace it yourself.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">No, AirTags are designed exclusively for Apple devices. Android users should look into alternatives like Tile or Chipolo.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">No, it doesn't have a built-in GPS chip. It uses Bluetooth to securely connect to any nearby Apple device in the world, which then updates the location on your map.</p>
