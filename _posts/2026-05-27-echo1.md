<h3 style="margin-bottom: 15px; font-size: 1.3rem;">🛒 Buying Guide: What to Consider Before Buying</h3>
<p style="line-height: 1.8; color: #444; margin-bottom: 20px;">Before purchasing a smart speaker, it's essential to evaluate your space and needs. First, consider your <strong>Wi-Fi connectivity</strong>. The Echo Dot requires a stable internet connection to process voice commands. Second, look at your <strong>existing ecosystem</strong>. If you already use Amazon Prime or Amazon Music, the integration is flawless. Finally, consider <strong>placement</strong>. Since it requires a power cord, you'll need a spot near a wall socket—making desks, nightstands, and kitchen counters the ideal locations.</p>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 15px; font-size: 1.3rem;">⚖️ Alternatives to Consider</h3>
<p style="line-height: 1.8; color: #444; margin-bottom: 40px;">While the Echo Dot is a fantastic device, you might be looking at competitors. The <strong>Google Nest Mini</strong> is a close rival, offering excellent Google Assistant integration, but it falls slightly short in bass response compared to the Echo Dot 5th Gen. Alternatively, the <strong>Apple HomePod Mini</strong> offers incredible sound, but comes at a significantly higher price point and restricts you to the Apple ecosystem. For the best balance of price, performance, and compatibility, the Echo Dot remains the top choice.</p>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 20px; font-size: 1.3rem;">❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<div style="margin-bottom: 50px; line-height: 1.7; font-size: 1rem;">
  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>1. Is the Echo Dot battery-powered?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">No, it is not a portable speaker. The device comes with a power adapter and needs to be plugged into a wall outlet to function.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>2. Can it control my normal (non-smart) AC, TV, or Geyser?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">Yes, absolutely! However, you will need to purchase a separate "Smart Plug" or an IR blaster. Once connected, Alexa can control your traditional appliances as well.</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>3. Does it understand Hindi or regional accents?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">Yes, Alexa has been highly optimized for the Indian audience. It understands both English and Hindi seamlessly, and can even comprehend mixed commands (Hinglish).</p>

  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>4. Can I connect my phone to it via Bluetooth without Wi-Fi?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 25px; color: #555;">While you can use the Echo Dot as a standard Bluetooth speaker, initial setup and voice commands strictly require an active Wi-Fi connection.</p>
  
  <p style="margin-bottom: 5px;"><strong>5. Can I use two Echo Dots together?</strong></p>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 20px; color: #555;">Yes! You can pair two Echo Dot devices together in the same room to create a wide, immersive stereo sound setup.</p>
